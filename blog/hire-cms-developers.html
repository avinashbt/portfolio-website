1. Why Hire a CMS Developer

A CMS lets marketers publish pages, blog posts and product updates without waiting on engineers. But the platform is only as good as the person who builds it. When you hire CMS developers you are paying for clean content models, fast templates and a site your team can actually manage.

Good CMS work also saves money long-term: fewer plugin conflicts, better security patches, and a backend that scales as your content library grows.

2. Skills to Look For

  • Platform depth: WordPress, Drupal, Joomla or headless CMS (Strapi, Sanity, Contentful)
  • Theme and plugin development — custom blocks, fields and reusable components
  • Frontend basics: HTML, CSS, JavaScript, responsive design, accessibility
  • Performance & security: caching, image optimization, backups, hardening
  • Integrations: REST APIs, forms, CRM, payment gateways, analytics
  • Migrations: moving content between platforms without losing SEO

Hiring tip: Ask whether they write custom code or only install plugins. Plugin-only developers ship faster but hit a ceiling on complex requirements.

5. How to Vet a Candidate

  • Open their live sites — check load speed on mobile, not screenshots
  • Ask for one GitHub repo or custom plugin they wrote themselves
  • Run a small paid test: add a custom content type and template
  • Confirm backups, staging environment and update process in writing

Red flags: "Guaranteed rankings", no live portfolio, quoting a 10-page site in three days, or no mention of backups and security.

6. Frequently Asked Questions

What does a CMS developer do?

They build and maintain your content management system — themes, plugins, custom content types and integrations — so your team can publish content without touching code.

How much does it cost to hire a CMS developer?

$15–40/hour in India, $35–75 in Eastern Europe and $60–130 in the US. Fixed-price CMS sites typically range from $2,000 to $20,000.

WordPress or headless CMS?

WordPress for blogs and fast publishing; headless CMS (Strapi, Sanity, Contentful) when the same content must feed a web app, mobile app and other channels.

How long does hiring take?

About two to three weeks: screening, interviews, a paid test project, then an offer.
